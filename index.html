Said before buying — these are conversion problems

Misleading product descriptions
what arrived wasn’t what was described.
— wisernotify.com
Prospects make decisions based on descriptions; discrepancies between expectation and reality lead to disappointment and distrust.
Hidden fees and unexpected costs
The user is enticed with a low advertised price. After investing time and effort, they discover unexpected fees and charges when they reach the checkout.
— deceptive.design
Prospects are confused and feel deceived by pricing that changes or includes undisclosed mandatory charges, making price comparison difficult and increasing perceived risk.
Confusing pricing and bundling
Other ways some businesses tend to overcomplicate pricing include hidden fees, inconsistent pricing across channels, frequent price changes, convoluted bundling, and confusing loyalty programs. These practices not only confound customers...
— netsuite.com
Complex or inconsistent pricing models, including convoluted bundling, make it hard for prospects to understand the true cost or value, leading to confusion and hesitation.
Unclear return and refund policies
If your refund policy is unclear, it can harm customer experience and make your roadmap for processing requests difficult.
— squarespace.com
Prospects feel risky about buying if they don't clearly understand the terms for returns or refunds, impacting their confidence in the purchase decision.
Lack of pre-purchase information
I read reviews about tools because I want to know if they are user-friendly, have bugs, or have helpful customer support. I’d like to know about the features, pricing, and perhaps, the platform's reliability.
— famewall.io
Prospects actively seek specific information like features, pricing, reliability, and issue resolution to evaluate a product and mitigate perceived risks before committing to a purchase.
